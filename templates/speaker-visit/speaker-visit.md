# Speaker visit plan

A visit from a speaker, elder, knowledge keeper or community member is part of the method. It connects learners with the people behind the language, not only the language itself. Plan it ahead so it is not the first thing dropped when time is short.

Fill this in before, during and after the visit. Keep it in your private content folder.

**This template is a planning aid only. It does not replace your community's protocols for inviting, honouring and thanking knowledge keepers. Follow those first, and ask your own people if you are unsure.**

## 3. Recording and use of what is shared

**Do not record without clear permission.** Before the visit, agree with the visitor:

- [ ] Whether it may be recorded (audio / video / notes / photographs) 
- [ ] What it may be used for (this class only; the program; public)
- [ ] Who may see or hear it
- [ ] How the visitor may change their mind later

If anything is recorded, add it to `sources.csv` and `audio.csv` with a `speaker_code`, the right `permitted_uses`, and the right `access` level. Do not put it in lessons until that is done.
